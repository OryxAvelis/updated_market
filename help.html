About AM MARKET

AM MARKET is a Moroccan online supermarket for groceries and everyday essentials.

Delivery

Delivery costs 20 DH and becomes free when your subtotal reaches 200 DH.

Payment

Choose cash on delivery, bank card, Wafacash or CashPlus during checkout.

Returns

Return eligible items within seven days. Keep the product and packaging in their original condition.

Frequently asked questions

When is delivery free?

Delivery is free when the product subtotal is 200 DH or more.

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse, add products and complete checkout as a guest.

Where are my cart and orders saved?

This demo stores them only in your current browser.
